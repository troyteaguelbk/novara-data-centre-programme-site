The programme

Start small. Build once. Scale with demand.

The first 5 MW customer module forms part of the permanent campus. Each later release uses the same operating model and advances only after demand, power, approvals, cost and delivery readiness are confirmed.

01 · DEMAND

Customer-led capacity

Capacity is released against customer requirements, contracting progress and an agreed occupancy path.

02 · DESIGN

One permanent plan

Early halls, common services and access are designed around the completed four-building campus.

03 · CAPITAL

Controlled releases

Every material draw follows a defined evidence gate, current sources and uses, and an updated risk position.

04 · POWER

Staged grid connection

Connection capacity, substations and augmentation are matched to the construction and customer sequence.

05 · DELIVERY

Repeatable modules

Standard technical blocks and live-site controls support a measured expansion from 5 MW to 350 MW.

06 · OPERATIONS

Built for service

Commissioning, maintenance, security and 24/7 operations are developed alongside the physical campus.

Casino, New South Wales

A concentrated four-building campus.

The current concept places four multistorey data-centre buildings and their supporting infrastructure within an identified campus area. The broader holdings provide room to resolve access, services, buffers, drainage and construction logistics through Phase 0 design.

The site has an established development and stakeholder history. The new data-centre use will follow the planning, technical and legal pathway confirmed through site-specific diligence.

Delivery path

From mobilisation to full service.

A six-month mobilisation programme converts the current project basis into site control, applications, studies, customer work, connection evidence, concept design, cost plans and delivery contracts.

Phase 0 · Months 0–6

Mobilise and prove

Secure conditional site control and complete the work needed for the development commitment.

Phase 1 · Months 6–18

Develop and contract

Advance land, approvals, power, fibre, design, demand and priced delivery packages.

Delivery · Months 18–80

Build in releases

Open the 5 MW pioneer and expand through 25, 50, 100, 250 and 350 MW.

Operations · From Month 36

Serve and optimise

Begin customer service while later buildings are delivered under live-campus controls.

Energy strategy

Connect the campus. Procure renewable supply.

The campus connection and the renewable-energy source are two related workstreams. The data centre connects to the grid at Casino. Renewable electricity may be contracted from qualifying off-site generators elsewhere on the relevant grid.

01

Core procurement path

Develop the staged grid connection and contract renewable electricity with the required matching, network, firming and customer pass-through arrangements.

02

Optional ownership path

If owned generation or storage improves the project, a separately funded PowerCo can develop assets on suitable land without setting the physical capacity of the data-centre campus.
